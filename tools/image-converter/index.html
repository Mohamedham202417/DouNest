How to use

  1. Drag your images (PNG, JPG, WebP) into the drop zone.
  2. Pick the target format: WebP, PNG or JPG.
  3. Set quality if you chose a lossy format like JPG or WebP.
  4. Press Convert and watch results appear with their new sizes.
  5. Download each image, or grab them all in one zip.

Detailed explanation

Image format conversion is a daily need from every direction: WebP for site speed (~30% smaller than JPG at equal quality), PNG for transparent logos, JPG for legacy compatibility — each format owns a scenario.

WebP is the hero of the modern story: supported in all modern browsers for years, measurably smaller, and promoted by Google itself in official performance guidance — converting your library is a direct investment in site speed.

The golden rule: transparency lives in PNG and WebP and dies in JPG; photographs prefer JPG/WebP while sharp graphics prefer PNG — respecting these prevents 90% of conversion surprises.

Conversion happens locally through the browser canvas — your personal and family photos never upload to any server, making it perfectly safe for heavy use.

Batch mode with ZIP download turns a hundreds-of-images task into one operation — built for anyone preparing an entire site's library at once.

FAQ

Which format is best for my site?

WebP by default, PNG for transparent logos, JPG as fallback via the HTML picture element.

Why did my image get bigger after conversion?

You probably converted a photograph to PNG — photos thrive in JPG/WebP and balloon in PNG.

Are image dimensions affected?

No — conversion preserves dimensions; use the dedicated resize tool to change them.
