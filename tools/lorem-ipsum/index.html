Detailed explanation

Lorem Ipsum is not random despite its look; it is scrambled Latin from a two-thousand-year-old essay by Cicero, adopted as an industry standard because its letter and word length distribution closely mirrors natural written language.

Its real design value is neutrality: readers focus on layout, dimensions and contrast instead of being distracted by dummy content — which is why design agencies refuse real text in prototypes.

Derived standards like realistic Arabic filler matter when designing Arabic interfaces — Arabic letterforms differ radically from Latin in height and connectivity, and testing with them reflects reality.

Practical bounds: generating up to 20 paragraphs covers full pages, with varied length patterns simulating realistic content densities.

Use it in prototypes, design tests and presentation drafts — then always replace it with real content before launch; published dummy text is the classic rookie mistake.
