How to use

  1. Pick the birth date from the picker (day, month, year).
  2. Pick the reference date or leave it as today by default.
  3. Read the age in years, months and days with full precision.
  4. See extra stats: days lived, hours, and your pet's equivalent age.
  5. Use comparison mode to find the exact age gap between two people.

Detailed explanation

Precise age calculation looks simple and hides famous subtleties: «I'm 25» may actually be 24 years, 11 months and 30 days — and the difference matters in official documents, visa applications, school and sports registrations.

The real complexity is variable month lengths: Feb 1 to Mar 1 is exactly one month in a common year, but counting the same span in days differs between leap and common years — correct algorithms handle these like official records do.

Official uses abound: insurance forms require age in days for some plans, retirement rights count in months, identity documents need auditing — day-level precision is not a luxury here.

Fun stats are more useful than they look: days lived explains the «time flies» feeling, and your 7-year-old cat equals roughly 44 human years — numbers connecting us to our time.

Pair-comparison mode serves practical cases: sibling age gaps, exact marriage duration, tenure at a job — all «age» from one date to another.

FAQ

Does it count leap years?

Yes — the real calendar including Feb 29, matching official records.

Are future dates supported?

Yes — put a future date in the «to» field to count the duration up to it.

Is my birth date saved?

No — calculation is memory-only; nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
