Productivity & Business — QR and Work Tools

Practical browser tools for QR codes, documents, signatures, invoices, resumes, and daily work.

Open the page in your browser to use the interactive workspace.

Practical tools for everyday productivity and business work: QR codes, signatures, invoices, resumes, timers, and notes. Start with the available tools; local input stays in your browser.

How to use the productivity and business center

  1. Choose the workflow that matches your task: QR, document, signature, invoice, or timer.
  2. Enter the required data and review the preview before generating a file or code.
  3. Download or copy the result, and keep an original when the information is business or official data.

Business workflows in one place

The center currently includes a QR Code Generator and QR Reader, with room for Invoice Generator, CV Builder, Barcode Generator, Signature Maker, WiFi QR, Pomodoro, and Notepad.

Preview before sharing

Review text, numbers, logos, and currency before downloading an invoice or document. QR tools do not validate the link or data you enter.

Your local input stays local

The current tools run in the browser and do not upload data to a DouNest server. Avoid storing sensitive data on shared devices and clear it when finished.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use QR for sensitive data?

You can generate the code locally, but do not place secrets or personal data in an image that will be shared or stored publicly.

Do QR tools validate links?

No. The generator encodes the text or URL you provide; test the destination yourself before publishing the code.

Are invoice details uploaded?

There is no active invoice tool in this batch; future local-first business tools should generate their files inside the browser.
