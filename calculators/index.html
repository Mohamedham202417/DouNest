Calculators — Age, BMI, Percentages and Units

Run everyday health, time, percentage, and unit calculations in your browser.

Open the page in your browser to use the interactive workspace.

Run quick calculations in your browser with age, BMI, percentage, and unit-conversion tools. Check units and inputs before relying on a result; informational calculations are not professional advice.

How to use the calculators

  1. Choose a calculator or converter from the cards.
  2. Enter values and confirm units, dates, and selected categories.
  3. Calculate, review rounding, and understand limitations before using the result for an important decision.

Calculations available

The center links to age, body-mass-index, percentage and percentage-change calculations, and common length, weight, volume, and temperature conversions. Open a standalone tool for its fields and instructions.

Interpreting results

Results may vary slightly because of rounding or date-entry conventions. BMI is a general screening measure, not a diagnosis, and should not be used alone for medical decisions. Check currencies and units when transferring calculated values.

Privacy

Values are calculated in your browser and are not sent to a DouNest server. Avoid shared devices when inputs reveal personal information.

Frequently asked questions

Does the BMI calculator diagnose my health?

No. BMI is a general screening measure and does not diagnose health on its own; consult a qualified professional for health decisions.

How do I calculate percentage change?

Use the percentage calculator with the original and new values; check the sign to distinguish an increase from a decrease.

Does the age calculator handle leap years?

Results depend on the dates entered and the tool's date conventions. Verify the birth date and time zone for dates near midnight.

Are my inputs sent to a server?

No. Calculations run locally in your browser and are not sent to a DouNest server.
