HEIC to JPG

Open iPhone HEIC photos and convert them to JPG.

Open iPhone HEIC photos and convert them to JPG.

How to use

  1. Drag HEIC/HEIF photos from your iPhone into the drop zone.
  2. Watch instant conversion of each image into compatible JPG.
  3. Review the preview and any size difference.
  4. Download images one by one or all together in a ZIP.
  5. Share them with any device or site without compatibility issues.

Detailed explanation

HEIC is the modern iPhone format: it compresses photos to roughly half of JPG's size at higher quality, but creates a compatibility dilemma: older Windows, Android, some websites and print services reject it outright — you send a family photo and it arrives as «unsupported file».

The tool decodes HEIC locally in your browser via the open heic2any library — no iPhone needed, no software installation, and your family photos' privacy is fully preserved because uploading simply does not exist.

The resulting JPG is 100% universally compatible: opens everywhere since 1990 — printing, Facebook, university admission portals, official documents all accept it without argument.

Output size will be larger than the original (HEIC is inherently efficient) — expected and healthy: you pay a little size for compatibility, and keeping the HEIC original for long-term storage is recommended practice.

Batch mode with ZIP matters specifically for iPhone users: Mom's camera transfers 60 trip photos at once and out comes one ZIP opening anywhere.

FAQ

Why does iPhone use HEIC at all?

To save storage: the same quality at roughly half the size — a smart internal trade-off that annoys sharing.

Does the image lose quality?

HEIC→JPG is a light re-encode; at high default quality the difference is practically invisible.

Are my photos uploaded to any server?

No — decoding and packing happen inside your browser; images never leave your device.
