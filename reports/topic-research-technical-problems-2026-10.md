# Research notes: technical-problem article candidates

**Checked:** 2026-10-15. Public search was used because no keyword-volume export or GSC/Ahrefs data was provided. Search result presence and official support coverage indicate plausible recurring intent; they do **not** establish monthly search volume or ranking difficulty.

## Candidate 1: A slow Windows 11 PC

**Potential English title:** *Slow Windows 11 PC? 10 Free Fixes to Improve Performance*

**Primary intent/phrase:** “how to speed up Windows 11” / “slow Windows PC fix.”

Microsoft's official performance guide recommends a diagnostic sequence covering Windows and driver updates, a malware scan, freeing storage with Storage Sense or Disk Cleanup, uninstalling unused apps, reviewing startup/background apps, adjusting visual effects and power mode, optimizing drives, checking resource use in Task Manager, and restarting. Microsoft cautions that Best performance mode increases power use, drains laptop batteries faster, and may create more heat. It also notes that older hardware can limit the gains.

PCMag's July 2026 guide confirms this is a current, editorially covered how-to intent; it recommends safe free software fixes while separately identifying hardware upgrades. The article should distinguish software cleanup from hardware limitations and avoid “one-click speed boost” promises or risky registry/optimizer downloads.

## Candidate 2: Wi-Fi connected, but no internet

**Potential English title:** *Wi-Fi Connected but No Internet? Free Fixes for Windows, iPhone, and Android*

**Primary intent/phrase:** “Wi-Fi connected but no internet.”

Microsoft's Windows troubleshooting guidance starts with checking network status and Airplane mode, forgetting/rejoining the network, and restarting modem/router. It documents a ping-to-default-gateway check to separate local Wi-Fi/router issues from downstream modem/ISP issues, and reserves Windows Network reset for a last resort.

Apple recommends checking whether other devices connect, checking router range/firmware and restarting router/modem; it warns Network Settings reset removes saved Wi-Fi networks/passwords and other network settings. Google Android recommends restarting the phone and switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data to compare; device menu names vary.

PCMag's July 2026 internet troubleshooting guide begins by testing another device and another website, then differentiates local device, network, and ISP issues. The article should put these low-risk diagnostic checks before resets, avoid guessing DNS values, and say when to contact the ISP or network administrator.

## SEO and editorial constraints

- Write English as the source version, then professionally localize the title, description, headings, steps, FAQs, and tags into the site's other nine locales: Arabic, French, Spanish, German, Turkish, Russian, Chinese, Hindi, and Portuguese.
- Use a separate crawlable URL per locale with self-canonical and reciprocal `hreflang`; a client-side language switch alone does not make translated versions independently indexable.
- Use a small set of topical tags, not hashtag stuffing. Hashtags are not a reliable Google ranking tactic.
- Do not claim measured search volumes, hands-on tests, guaranteed speed gains, or universal fixes.
