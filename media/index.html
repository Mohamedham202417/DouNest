Video & Audio — Local Media Workflows

Prepare a privacy-first home for video and audio conversion, trimming, compression, and recording.

Open the page in your browser to use the interactive workspace.

A focused home for video and audio conversion, compression, trimming, recording, and export. Processing stays local in the browser whenever the selected operation supports it; files are not uploaded to DouNest.

How to use the video and audio center

  1. Choose the video or audio workflow and add a file from your device.
  2. Select quality, trim points, or an output format, then review the expected size.
  3. Run the operation, preview the result, and download it after checking quality and compatibility.

Planned media workflows

This center is prepared for Video to MP3, video compression, video or audio trimming, Video to GIF, audio conversion, voice recording, and screen recording.

Privacy and performance

Media operations use browser capabilities and ffmpeg.wasm where appropriate. Large processing libraries may download on first use, and large files need enough device memory.

Verify the output

Video and audio encoding can change quality, metadata, or compatibility. Keep the original and open the exported file before sharing it.

Frequently asked questions

Are my video files uploaded?

Files are not sent to a DouNest server when the operation runs locally. Processing libraries may download from a content-delivery network on first use.

Why does media conversion take time?

Encoding uses CPU and memory; resolution, duration, and file size all affect processing time.

Does conversion preserve the original quality?

Not for every format. Review the quality settings and inspect the output before removing the original.
