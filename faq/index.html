Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about file conversion, PDF, images, and privacy; tools process files locally where supported.

Format Conversion

How do I convert an image to a PDF on my phone?

Open the Image to PDF tool in DouNest from your mobile browser, pick images from your gallery (multiple allowed), arrange their order, then press Convert. A single PDF is generated and downloaded right on your device. Everything happens inside the browser — your photos are never uploaded to any server.

How can I compress a PDF without losing quality?

Use the Compress PDF tool in DouNest: pick your file, choose a compression level (low for higher quality, high for smaller size), then download the result. Images and redundant data are re-encoded right inside the browser. Text stays sharp while heavy images shrink dramatically.

How do I open an iPhone HEIC file and convert it to JPG?

HEIC is the default photo format on modern iPhones and many devices can't open it. Solution: open the HEIC to JPG tool in DouNest, drop or pick your photos, and get universally compatible JPG copies in seconds — no software, no uploads.

What is the difference between JPG, PNG and WebP?

JPG suits photos (small size, no transparency). PNG keeps sharp edges and supports transparency — ideal for logos and screenshots. WebP is the modern format: smaller than JPG, supports transparency like PNG, and works in all modern browsers. Use the Image Converter tool in DouNest to switch between them instantly.

How do I convert Excel to CSV or JSON?

Open the Excel to CSV or Excel to JSON tool in DouNest, pick your XLSX/XLS file, choose the sheet, and download the result. Powered by SheetJS running locally in your browser, so even sensitive spreadsheet data never leaves your device.

How do I convert a Word document to PDF for free?

Use the Word to PDF tool in DouNest: pick your .docx, its content is rendered into a formatted page, then the print dialog opens — choose Save as PDF. No limits, no watermarks, no sign-up, unlike most freemium converters.

How do I extract a table from PDF into Excel?

Fastest path: if your PDF has real text (not scanned), extract it with the PDF to Text tool; if it's scanned, run it through the OCR tool. Paste the result into the CSV to Excel tool to get a clean table you can download as XLSX.

Image Editing

How do I remove an image background for free without uploading it?

The Remove Background tool in DouNest runs an open-source AI model directly in your browser: pick an image, wait a few seconds for automatic subject isolation, then download a transparent PNG. The model (~40MB) downloads only on first use and is cached afterwards. Your photos never touch any server.

How do I extract text from an image (OCR) in Arabic?

Open the Image to Text (OCR) tool in DouNest, pick your image (screenshot or document photo), choose the language — Arabic, English, or both — and press Extract. The engine is the open-source Tesseract OCR supporting 100+ languages. Copy the result or download it as a text file.

How do I shrink image sizes for faster sharing or uploads?

Two options in DouNest: Image Compressor reduces file size via quality tuning, and Image Resizer changes actual dimensions (by width or percentage) while keeping the aspect ratio. Both support JPG, PNG and WebP output and handle batches of images locally in your browser.

How do I read a QR code from an image?

Open the QR Reader (from image) tool in DouNest and pick any picture containing a code — a screenshot or camera photo. The content (URL, text, phone number) is extracted instantly and can be copied. No external app, no live camera needed — great for inspecting links before opening them.

PDF Files

How do I merge multiple PDF files into one?

Use the Merge PDF tool in DouNest: pick two or more files, arrange their order with the move buttons, then press Merge. One combined PDF is downloaded with pages in the right order. Powered by the open-source pdf-lib library, fully local.

How do I split a PDF or extract specific pages?

Open the Split PDF tool in DouNest and type page ranges like 1-3,5,8-10. Choose a single output with the selected pages, or split every page into its own file (delivered inside one ZIP). Perfect for extracting a book chapter or sharing just part of a contract.

How do I convert a PDF to JPG images?

Use the PDF to Images tool in DouNest: pick a file and every page is rendered to a JPG with adjustable quality. A single page downloads directly; multiple pages arrive as one ZIP. Built on Mozilla's pdf.js, running entirely in your browser.

How do I rotate pages in a PDF that show sideways?

Open the Rotate PDF tool in DouNest, choose the file and the angle (90, 180 or 270 degrees), optionally limit it to specific page ranges, then download the fixed copy. Rotation is saved permanently into the file so pages display upright everywhere.

How do I pack several files into one ZIP archive?

Use the ZIP Files tool in DouNest: pick any number of files of any type (images, documents, PDFs), optionally name the archive, and get a single ZIP. Powered by JSZip inside your browser — especially handy on phones where creating archives is usually hard.

Privacy & Security

Are online file converter websites safe? What is the risk of uploading files?

Most traditional converter sites upload your files to their servers for processing — your documents and personal photos pass through third-party machines where they may be retained, and can be intercepted on public networks. DouNest takes a different approach: every tool runs inside your browser via JavaScript/WebAssembly, so not a single byte of your files ever leaves your device. You can even disconnect from the internet after loading the page and the tools keep working.

Do DouNest tools work offline?

Yes. DouNest is a Progressive Web App (PWA): after your first visit the tools are cached on your device and you can install it as an app from your browser menu and use it offline. Tools relying on large downloadable libraries — like background removal and OCR — need a connection only on first use to fetch the model, then work offline.

Is DouNest really free? What's the catch?

Yes — every tool is completely free with no daily limits or size caps, because processing happens on your device and costs us no server time. The site is funded by unobtrusive ads, clearly disclosed affiliate links (AI Directory and Rare Tools pages), and an optional support button. No sign-up, no email, no watermarks.

Where are my files processed? Do you keep copies?

All files are processed locally in your browser's memory via JavaScript and WebAssembly. We keep no copies because your files never reach us — there is no server receiving them in the first place. The only data stored is your preferences (language, theme, favorites) on your own device, plus optional anonymous analytics you can disable in config.
