  5. Use the alpha slider to add transparency (alpha) to the color.

Detailed explanation

Web colors speak three languages: HEX is the dialect of CSS and HTML, RGB is the physical model of red, green and blue light, and HSL is the human model of hue, saturation and lightness — each fits a different working context.

HSL in particular is a golden key for systematic design: fix the hue and move lightness to auto-generate a harmonious color family — entire visual identities are built from one color this way.

Alpha (transparency) is stored as RGBA, HSLA or #RRGGBBAA, the foundation of modern overlays, shadows and glows in interfaces.

The tool converts in both directions instantly with validation: a bad paste does not break anything, it politely asks for the right syntax.

Front-end developers use it dozens of times a day: paste a color from Figma, convert it to a CSS variable, generate its shades — the whole loop completes here in seconds.

FAQ

What is the practical difference between HEX and RGB?

No difference in the resulting color — only notation; HEX is shorter to write, RGB easier to compute with.

How do I generate shades of one color?

Convert to HSL, keep hue and saturation fixed, and move lightness — every value gives a harmonious shade.

Does the tool support transparency?

Yes — through the alpha slider generating RGBA and 8-digit HEX values.
