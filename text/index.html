Text Tools — Clean, Convert and Analyze

Clean text, convert Markdown to HTML, create slugs, and count words in your browser.

افتح الصفحة في متصفحك لاستخدام مساحة العمل التفاعلية.

A practical text toolkit for word and character counts, list cleanup, URL slugs, and Markdown-to-HTML. Paste or load text, choose a task, then copy or download the result.

How to use the text center

  1. Paste text or load a supported TXT or Markdown file.
  2. Choose an operation and set options such as deduplication or slug separator.
  3. Run it, then copy or download and review the result before publishing.

Text tools available

The center includes text statistics, Markdown-to-HTML, list cleanup with duplicate removal and sorting options, and slug generation with optional Arabic letters. Linked standalone pages provide word counts, case conversion, and placeholder text.

Review before publishing

Cleanup can reorder or remove lines you consider duplicates; check the deduplication and sorting settings. Markdown-to-HTML creates structured markup, but review links, tags, and quotations in the application that will display it.

Privacy

Text input is processed in your browser and is not sent to a DouNest server. Avoid entering secrets on shared devices and clear the input when finished.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text sent to a server?

No. This center processes text in your browser and does not send it to a DouNest server.

What is a URL slug?

A readable part of a page URL. Keep it concise, separate words with hyphens, and check your site's URL rules.

Can I remove duplicate list entries?

Yes. Choose list cleanup and enable duplicate removal; you can also choose case sensitivity and sorting.

Is Markdown-to-HTML safe?

The tool produces HTML from Markdown. Review the output and apply your platform's sanitization rules before rendering untrusted content.
