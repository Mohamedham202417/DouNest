Favicon Generator

Turn an image into multi-size site icons and an ICO file.

🔒 Files are processed locally when the tool supports it media

Turn an image into multi-size site icons and an ICO file.

How to use

  1. Choose a square or near-square image.
  2. Generate a ZIP containing multiple PNG sizes, an ICO and an HTML snippet.
  3. Copy the icons to your site and update the paths in the snippet.

Detailed explanation

The image is center-cropped to a square and resized to 16, 32, 48, 180, 192 and 512 pixels.

Some platforms need extra sizes or manifest files; check your hosting platform's requirements.

FAQ

Is my ZIP uploaded to DouNest?

No. The icons and archive are created locally in your browser.
